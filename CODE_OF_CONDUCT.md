# Code of conduct

Daybook follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and experience level, and keep criticism about the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues,
pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment
or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block people who don't
follow this code.
